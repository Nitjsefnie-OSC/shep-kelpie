# The triage pass

One issue's first pass (#887): the call, the post, the labels, the duplicate close and the agent-ready branch for the maintainer's own issue the pass is sure of (#891). The settings and pick rule are in `triage.md`.

- `Runner::triage_due` runs from `dispatch` after `contribution_due` and before the board's pick, so it needs the board open and a free slot. It takes the issue the verb queued, then the oldest in `triage.due`, one at a time, and starts nothing while draining or where no App covers the repo. Each issue is tried once a poll. `src/runner/triage.rs`
- Before an issue's first call its comments are read once (`Forge::issue_comments`). A comment by the App's login holding `<!-- kelpie:triage -->` records the issue with no call, and one holding `<!-- kelpie:triage duplicate #n -->` closes it as a duplicate of `n`. Only the last line's marker counts: model-worded text can hold marker-shaped text of its own. The marker in anyone else's comment counts for nothing. The verb's issue skips the first read, and is read after a post try past its old record's comment. The call's ledger line is keyed apart from any work item's (`flight/triage.rs` `KEY`).
- The call is a flight: `src/runner/flight/triage.rs`, one at a time on its own thread, `CEILING` 15 minutes, `News::Triaged`. `slot_issues` counts it and a drain lists it in `calls` as role `triager`. It runs `triage.agent` in a `worktree::view` of `origin/main` at `<project>/triage/<n>/`, removed after, with no repo plugins.
- The prompt holds the issue and the open issues, all `fenced`. Code keeps a related number only if the listing holds it, a file only if the view holds it, cuts `open` at `max_questions`, and turns a `P0` into an open question. `src/runner/triage/research.rs`
- The representative words the facts (`Runner::represent_issue`), code appends the marker, `Voice::post` posts it. Then the type and priority the repo has (`Forge::repo_labels`), a priority looked for under the board's own name `priority: P2` first and under the bare `P2` only on a repo that names its priorities that way (`board::repo_priority`), then `needs-triage`, made if missing. A label the repo has under neither name is not put on. `src/runner/triage/comment.rs`
- `triage.issues` holds `{issue, comment, step, labels, duplicate_of, ready_body, seen}`, `step` being `commented`, `closed`, `labelled`, `body-written`, `ready` or `done`, `labels` the type and priority still owed, `duplicate_of` the item a closed duplicate names, `ready_body` the body owed over the issue's and `seen` the labels the issue carried when its pass began, each left out while empty. The post is the one write with no save before it: a post whose record did not save is caught by the marker, which is read again after every post. Each later write follows a save of its step. The last 200 `done` records are kept (`TRIAGED_KEPT`), keeping every record that closed a duplicate: it carries no label and keeps a reopened one out of `triage.due`. A poll finishes a record short of `done` or `ready`.
- A failure is told once in the log and tried at the next poll. Five give the issue up until a restart. A busy forge does not count. A research that read but could not be posted is kept in memory and posted again at the next poll.
- The ledger line is role `triager`, kind `triage`, with the issue. A body edit is its own line, kind `edit-issue`. `src/forge_ledger/kind.rs`

## A duplicate

- The research also answers whether something covers it. The prompt carries the open pull requests beside the open issues, `fenced` and without the issue itself: number, title, and the issues its body names with `Resolves`, `Fixes` or `Closes`, from `Forge::open_pulls`: the keyword is a word of its own, takes a colon before the number and markup before itself, and a number ends at whitespace or punctuation. The reply gains `addressed_by`: null or `{number, kind, why}`, `kind` being `issue` or `pull_request`. `src/runner/triage.rs`, `src/adapters/gh/issues.rs`, `agents/triager.md`
- Code keeps it only when its number is one the call was shown, the forge still lists it as open and of the kind it was given as, and it is not the issue itself. What it cannot keep is dropped and told in the log, and the issue is triaged as any other; a `kind` that is neither word is one of them, quoted in the note. An unreadable listing fails the pass. The cover is read again right before the post, since wording can take minutes: a cover that went is not posted, and the issue is triaged at the next poll. `src/runner/triage/addressed.rs`
- A kept one ends the pass: the representative words a short comment naming the other item as `#n` and the reason, code appends the marker, `Voice::post` posts it, the record saves `closed`, and only then `Forge::close_issue_as(repo, number, IssueCloseReason)` closes it for reason `duplicate`, through `gh api`'s `state_reason`. `close_issue` is unchanged, and no label goes on a closed duplicate. `src/runner/triage/comment.rs`, `src/ports/issue.rs`
- A restart at `closed` closes it and posts nothing again. `status` says `closed as a duplicate of #n` once the close is done and `to close as a duplicate of #n` while it is owed. An author who reopens one is triaged again only through `shep kelpie triage <n>`: its record keeps it out of `triage.due`.

## The maintainer's issue, made agent-ready

- Sure means a type, a priority, no open question and a `body` `issues::has_criteria` accepts that names `Touches:`. Only an issue whose author is `Runner::maintainer()`, ignoring case, is made ready: an outside issue never does. `src/runner/triage/ready.rs`
- The comment is worded as any other, its facts saying kelpie will rewrite the body and label it `ready-for-agent`. The promise is made only where kelpie can keep it: not while a hand triage label stands, and not for a body the local guard would refuse. `src/runner/triage/comment.rs` (`facts`), `ready.rs` (`readiness`)
- `Voice::edit_issue_body` replaces the issue's body, the title as it stands: `AppVoice` sends the REST issue edit, `PATCH /repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues/{number}` naming only `body`, through `GuardedVoice`, `PacedVoice` and `RecordedVoice`. A body naming a path on this machine is dropped before the comment is worded, on the same `LocalPaths` check every App post goes through, and the issue ends at `needs-triage` with its body as its author wrote it. A refusal kelpie cannot read beforehand, a `publish` hook, is said on the issue in a short notice, since the comment promised the rewrite. `src/ports/voice.rs`, `src/github/voice.rs`, `src/github/api.rs`, `src/ports/local_paths.rs`
- After the comment comes the body edit (`body-written`), then the type and priority the repo has, then the `needs-triage` an earlier pass left off, then `ready-for-agent` last (`ready`), made if the repo lacks it: an issue is not both ready for the board and waiting for the maintainer. The `labelled` step is unused here. `src/runner/triage/ready.rs`
- A refused edit is told once and tried at the next poll, under the five-failure give-up. Once `body-written` is saved the body is never written again: a restart at `commented` holds it in `ready_body`, and a restart at `body-written` or `ready` labels, or does nothing.
- A triage label put on by hand stands, as `src/runner/flight/label.rs` does for `agent:` labels. The labels the issue carried when its pass began are read against the issue before the comment is worded, before the body write and before the labelling, so one that came by at any point ends the pass at `done` with the body kept and nothing more labelled. That reading is the record's `seen`, so a pass that resumes after a restart still knows what was already there and takes none of kelpie's own labels for the maintainer's. At the labelling step `ready-for-agent` is the label kelpie is writing, so on the issue there it is kelpie's own and the step finishes; any other triage label there stands. `src/runner/triage/ready.rs`
- `ready` counts as finished: pruned like `done`, no new pass. The board takes the issue as any `ready-for-agent` issue, its `agent:` label from `src/runner/flight/label.rs` as ever.
