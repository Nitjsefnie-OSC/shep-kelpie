# Triage

Kelpie's triage of issues other people open (#887): the settings, the agent, the pick rule, the verb and `status`. One issue's pass, its duplicate close and its agent-ready branch are in `triage-pass.md`, the author's round, its ask and its own session in `triage-author.md`, and the classifier check in `triage-spam.md`.

- `[app.dogs.kelpie.triage]`, an absent table meaning `pick = "off"`, with `deny_unknown_fields`. `src/settings/triage.rs`
  - `pick`: `off` (default), `maintainer` (only the maintainer's issues) or `all`
  - `wait_days`: whole days above 0, default 7
  - `max_questions`: 1 to 10, default 10, refused above 10 by `Triage::check`.
  - `per_day` (default 10), `per_author_per_day` (default 2), `min_account_days` (default 30) and `max_body_kb` (default 20): the four triage limits, each `0` for off, each a `u32` refused by name for a negative or non-number value. `src/settings/tests/triage.rs`
  - `agent`: an agent file whose role is `triager`, default `triager`. `Settings::triager` checks it.
- `[app.dogs.kelpie.triage.spam]`, absent meaning no check: `gateway`, `path` and `threshold` (default 0.8, refused outside 0 to 1). `triage-spam.md`
- `reread` keeps a running `triage` waiting for the next start, as it does `contributions`. `src/runner/reread.rs`
- Agent file `triager.md`, role `triager`, `Expert` frontmatter, shipped on Claude Code and written by `add`. It researches an issue against a clean checkout and reports in one JSON object: `type`, `priority`, `related`, `addressed_by`, `files`, `tests`, `criteria`, `open` and `body`, the last a rewrite of the issue text for an agent. The issue text is data. `agents/triager.md`
- Every harness gives the triager the reviewer's read-only reach (`Tools::Review`), per `harness.triager` in `docs/parity.toml`.
- `Role::Triager` in `src/agents.rs` and `crate::ports::Role`. No work item records its calls (`src/work_item/spend.rs`, `tally.rs`). The code graph reaches it when on (`reads_code`, `src/runner/code_graph.rs`), with its own index (`ProjectPaths::triage_graph`), removed when the call ends. `CallKind::Triage` gives it a `triager` total in `usage`, apart from work items. `src/usage.rs`
- `Runner::open` checks the settings in `src/runner/open_checks.rs`: while `triage.pick` is not `off` the App must cover the repo (as `contributions.pick` does), and `triage.agent` and `agents.representative` must name files with the right role; under `off` none is read.
- `OpenIssue` carries `author`, `author_is_bot`, `author_association`, `labels` and `created`. `author_association` is how the forge counts whoever opened it to the repo (`AuthorAssociation`), answered only by the REST listing: gh 2.96 refuses `authorAssociation` from `gh issue list --json` (`docs/facts/what-ghs-listings-answer.md`). REST answers pull requests beside issues and no filter that separates them, so its pages are read until 1000 issues are in hand or it runs out, bounded at `LIST_PAGES` calls each cut at 1000 keeping the newest. `src/ports/issue.rs`, `src/adapters/gh/issues.rs`. `Forge::account_created(login)` reads when an account was made.
- `ProjectState.triage { since }` (version 60) is when `pick` went past `off`, left out while unset. Only an issue created after it is triaged: no reach back over the backlog. `Runner::open` alone sets it, when `pick` is not `off` and none is saved, and clears it under `off`. `src/runner/triage.rs` (`settle_since`), `src/state/triage.rs`
- A triage record carries `at` (when it was made), `author`, `held` (the limit that held it) and `calls`, the moment of every triager call its issue made, each saved when its call ends, oldest first, the last `TRIAGE_CALLS_KEPT` kept (state version 70). A call counts when it reached a model, whatever its reply came to. A record a limit held, and one made from a triage comment found on the issue, hold no call. A record saved before them reads as none of the three, so it counts toward none. A call on an issue with no record yet, which is what a first pass leaves until its comment posts, is saved under `triage.unrecorded` with its author, which a record then takes. `TriageState::prune` keeps a record holding a call ahead of one holding none, so a burst of holds cannot push the counted records out. `src/state/triage.rs`
- The four limits are checked in order before the spam check and the research call, none a model call. `src/runner/triage/limits.rs`
  - `per_day`: at most this many calls in any 24 hours, counted from the calls the records save and those saved for an issue with no record yet, which a restart does not lose. Past it an issue waits in `triage.due` for the next free place and is never dropped. A cap above `MOST_PER_DAY` (200, the same as `TRIAGED_KEPT`) is refused at the settings check: that many finished records, a call-holding one ahead of one holding none, is all a 24 hour count can see.
  - `per_author_per_day`: the same count over one author's issues, logins compared ignoring case. Past it the issue gets `needs-triage` with no call and no comment, and its record says which limit held it.
  - `min_account_days`: an author younger than this whose `author_association` is `NONE`, `FIRST_TIMER` or `FIRST_TIME_CONTRIBUTOR` is held the same way. The age is read once per author per start; an age the forge cannot say holds nothing.
  - `max_body_kb`: the body the call is given is cut at that many kilobytes, on no character, with a line in the fence naming the whole body's size. The title is never cut.
  - The maintainer's own issues and one `shep kelpie triage` asked for skip all four and the spam check, which is why `triage_job` is told whether the limits reach it. A hold is final for that issue: its label and its record keep it out of the due list, and only the verb reaches it again.
  - The author's cap and the account's age decide which of an author's issues kelpie takes on, so they reach an issue that has spent no call. An issue kelpie is already working on is gated by the day's cap alone: a hold is final, and calls that came to nothing are no reason to label an issue over for good.
  - The author's round is gated by the day's cap as the first pass is, since its calls spend the same budget: a round call with no free place waits for the next poll, which reads the round from the records again. It is never held: a hold would label over an issue already triaged.
  - An author the listing gives no login for, which an account deleted since the issue went up leaves, counts with no one: their records share no bucket, so they cannot spend each other's allowance.
  - A record that names no author, which a file saved before the caps leaves, gets it from the listing at the next poll: the author is what the cap counts by and what separates the maintainer's issues from everyone else's.
- The pick rule runs once a `BOARD_POLL` while `pick` is not `off`, over `open_issues`. An issue is untouched when it is open and carries none of `TRIAGE_LABELS`, no bot opened it (the App included), it has no record, it came after `triage.since`, and under `maintainer` `Runner::maintainer()` opened it, ignoring case.
- `status` lists them under `triage.due`, oldest first (number, title, author, author_association, created), left out when none; plain `status` adds a `triage due` line. The list lives in memory, rebuilt by the next poll. `src/runner/triage.rs`, `src/runner/trigger.rs`, `src/flock/control/plain.rs`
- `status` says the day's cap as `triage.daily` (`used`, `cap`, `opens`, `wait`) while triage and the cap are on, and plain `status` says the count and when the next place opens. A held record is said in the plain block in `TriageHeld::said`'s words.
- A listing that cannot be read, or a maintainer that cannot be named, fails no poll.
- `shep kelpie triage <n>` queues `n` ahead of `triage.due`, past `since` and the author test. Refused when `pick` is `off`, when `n` is not open or carries a triage label, or when its call is in flight. A recorded issue is triaged again, its record replaced when the new comment posts.
- `status` lists the call in flight under `triage.running` and the records under `triage.issues`, a closed one with its `duplicate_of`.
