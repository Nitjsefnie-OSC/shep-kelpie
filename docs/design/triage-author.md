# The author's round

An open question goes to the issue's author before it goes to the maintainer (#887, #892, #893): one round, on GitHub or in the author's own terminal, and `needs-triage` for what is left. The settings, the pick rule, the verb and `status` are in `triage.md`; the pass, its post and its labels are in `triage-pass.md`.

- `Runner::askable` is who is asked: the maintainer on their own issue, and any author under `pick = "all"`. Anyone else ends at `needs-triage` as before. `src/runner/triage/ask.rs`
- Asked and not sure: the comment's facts end asking the author to settle the open questions, `github`, `local` or `none`, and no status label goes on while the triage waits. `Runner::finish_triage` returns at a record whose `asked`, `questions` or `local` are open, and the type and priority the pass owed wait in its `labels`. `src/runner/triage/comment.rs`
- `triage.issues` gains `asked {at, asks}` and `questions {comment, at}` (state version 67) and `local {at, body}` (state version 68), each left out while unset. `at` is when the ask, the questions or the prompt went up and is what silence is measured from; `asks` is 1, or 2 once the plain ask went up, and is left out while 1. `crates/core/src/state/triage.rs`
- Hearing: `read_threads` reads while any triage waits, `Runner::triage_waits` putting each wait's start into the read's `since` and holding the read's floor there while a wait is open, so a busy forge or a held slot does not drop the reply, and `Runner::hear_triage` keeps only what the issue's own author wrote on that issue after the wait began, by login through `plain_login`. A bot, the App's own post and anyone else's words are dropped. The login is the pass's, taken from the open listing each poll so a restart has it. `src/runner/thread_replies.rs`, `src/runner/triage/heard.rs`
- Reading: `src/runner/representative/reading.rs` is the one shape a reply is read through. `kind` words what the call is for from the words the caller gave, and `read` keeps only one of them or `other`; the reply text is fenced by `represent_issue`, so it is data and never instructions. `contributions/release.rs` reads `yes`, `no` or `other` through it, and triage reads `github`, `local`, `none` or `other`. A word read for a comment is kept in the pass, so a post that failed is not paid for again.
- Each reply found is read, oldest first, and the first word kelpie can read is what it acts on. A reply that is none of the words gets one plain comment asking again (`<!-- kelpie:triage ask -->`, `Runner::triage_ask_again`), which sets `asks` to 2 and moves `at` to itself. A second reply kelpie cannot read counts as `none`.
- `none`, or no word `triage.wait_days` after the ask: the labels the pass owed go on, then `needs-triage`, and nothing is posted, so the triage comment stays the maintainer's starting point. `Runner::triage_none`
- `github`: one comment (`<!-- kelpie:triage questions -->`) whose lead-in the representative words and whose questions kelpie's code lists from the triager's `open`, at most `max_questions`, numbered, each with its recommended answer, in the vendored grilling skill's shape (`skills/skills/productivity/grilling`) without its bold. `Runner::post_questions`. A `github` whose questions a restart lost is researched again before anything is posted (`Purpose::Questions`).
- The author's comments after the questions are the answers. A second triager call (`Purpose::Answers`) reads the issue, the questions and the answers, each fenced, and answers again. The maintainer's own issue takes the agent-ready branch of `triage-pass.md`. An outside issue gets what is settled written under its author's own text in its body, never in place of it: the `answers` step, with the whole body in `ready_body`. `Runner::settled_answers`, `Runner::answers_write`. What is still open is listed in that comment, and there is no second round. A body the forge cannot be read for is not stood for by a comment and labels: the pass runs again. A write a hook refused keeps the type and priority the pass found.
- No answer `triage.wait_days` after the questions ends the way `none` does.
- `local`: one comment, its last line `<!-- kelpie:triage local body:<hash> -->`, the representative's lead-in and a fenced block kelpie's code builds: `/grill-with-docs Issue <owner/repo>#<n>: <title>.`, reading the issue with `gh issue view <n> --comments`, the files the triage comment named, the open questions each with what the research recommends, and the write-back that is how kelpie learns what was settled: rewrite the body with acceptance criteria and a `Touches:` list, and comment the decisions made. The title, every file and every question with its recommended answer lose backticks and line breaks, so none of them can close the block or stand as a line of the prompt. The comment says where the skill is: `grill-with-docs` from mattpocock/skills, kelpie's vendored copy at `skills/skills/engineering/grill-with-docs`. `Runner::post_local_prompt`, `src/runner/triage/local.rs`. A `local` whose questions and files a restart lost are researched again before anything is posted (`Purpose::LocalPrompt`).
- `local {at, body}` keeps the body kelpie last saw as a FNV-1a hash of its trimmed text, so a wait begun on one body is never read against another. Kelpie's own body writes set it (`Runner::triage_body_written`, called where `Runner::answers_write` and `Runner::ready_write` write), so a write of kelpie's own is not the author's edit. The prompt comment's last line carries the hash it went up against, so a prompt that posted before a crash leaves the round measured against that body and not the one the forge holds when kelpie comes back: `Runner::app_comment_after` finds the comment, its hash is read off it, and the wait is timed from the forge's own time for that comment rather than from the restart. A prompt kelpie finds on the issue is taken up before a pass that now finds no question left is ended as `none`. The hash is taken from a read made just before the post, since the wording call under it can run for minutes. The ask comes off when the prompt posts, and the prompt is what its `wait_days` is measured from. `src/runner/triage/local.rs`
- Each `BOARD_POLL`, `Runner::watch_local_waits` reads each issue waiting on `local` through `Tickets::issue`. A body whose hash differs starts the triager call again on the new text (`Purpose::LocalRead`), and its comment ends the round: the maintainer's own issue the pass is sure of takes the agent-ready branch of `triage-pass.md`, any other gets `needs-triage`, and what is still open goes to the maintainer with no further ask. The wait stays open until that comment lands, so a restart between the edit and its call reads the body again and starts the call again. No read runs under a call in flight on the issue. That call is made on the body this read saw, and `Runner::local_read_stale` reads the body again before its comment goes up: a body rewritten a second time under the call posts nothing and writes no body, and is owed again on the newer text.
- No edit `triage.wait_days` after the prompt ends the way `none` does, and `Runner::triage_none` clears `local` with the rest.
- A triage label put on by hand, or the issue closed, ends the wait with no post and no label: `Runner::settle_triage_waits` reads the listing each poll, and `Runner::triage_wait_stood` saves the record `done` with nothing owed.
- The wait holds no slot and no call of its own. The call it starts is taken from `Runner::triage_round_due`, after the same pick as the first, and takes a slot as the first does.
- No wait is settled, read or asked for under a triager call in flight on the same issue, and a given-up issue is neither heard from nor settled until kelpie restarts. `Runner::answers_round` returning none is no call. `src/runner/triage/ask.rs`, `src/runner/triage/heard.rs`
