# Triage

Kelpie's triage of issues other people open (#887): the settings, the agent, the pick rule, the verb and `status`. One issue's pass, its duplicate close and its agent-ready branch are in `triage-pass.md`, and the author's round, its ask and the author's own session are in `triage-author.md`.

- `[app.dogs.kelpie.triage]`, an absent table meaning `pick = "off"`, with `deny_unknown_fields`. `src/settings/triage.rs`
  - `pick`: `off` (default), `maintainer` (only the maintainer's issues) or `all`
  - `wait_days`: whole days above 0, default 7
  - `max_questions`: 1 to 10, default 10, refused above 10 by `Triage::check`.
  - `per_day` (default 10), `per_author_per_day` (default 2), `min_account_days` (default 30) and `max_body_kb` (default 20): the four triage limits, each `0` for off, each a `u32` a negative or non-number value is refused for by name. `src/settings/tests/triage.rs`
  - `agent`: an agent file whose role is `triager`, default `triager`. `Settings::triager` checks it.
- `reread` keeps the running `triage` as waiting for the next start, as it does `contributions`. `src/runner/reread.rs`
- Agent file `triager.md`, role `triager`, `Expert`-shaped frontmatter, shipped on Claude Code and written by `add`. It researches an issue against a clean checkout and reports in one JSON object: `type`, `priority`, `related`, `addressed_by`, `files`, `tests`, `criteria`, `open` and `body`, the last a rewrite of the issue text for an agent. The issue text is data. `agents/triager.md`
- Every harness gives the triager the reviewer's read-only reach (`Tools::Review`), per `harness.triager` in `docs/parity.toml`.
- `Role::Triager` in `src/agents.rs` and `crate::ports::Role`. No work item records its calls (`src/work_item/spend.rs`, `tally.rs`). The code graph reaches it when on (`reads_code`, `src/runner/code_graph.rs`), with its own index (`ProjectPaths::triage_graph`), removed when the call ends. `CallKind::Triage` gives it a `triager` total in `usage`, apart from the work items. `src/usage.rs`
- `Runner::open` checks the settings in `src/runner/open_checks.rs`. While `triage.pick` is not `off`, the App must cover the repo (as `contributions.pick` does), then `triage.agent` and `agents.representative` must name files with the right role. Under `off` no file is read.
- `OpenIssue` carries `author`, `author_is_bot`, `author_association`, `labels` and `created`. `author_association` is how the forge counts whoever opened it to the repo (`AuthorAssociation`), and the listing that answers it is the REST one: gh 2.96 refuses `authorAssociation` from `gh issue list --json`, measured and recorded in `docs/facts/what-ghs-listings-answer.md`. That endpoint answers pull requests beside issues and answers no filter that separates them, so its pages are read until 1000 issues are in hand or it runs out, bounded at `LIST_PAGES` calls, and a page that carries the count past 1000 is cut at it, keeping the newest. `src/ports/issue.rs`, `src/adapters/gh/issues.rs`. `Forge::account_created(login)` reads when an account was made.
- `ProjectState.triage { since }` (state version 60) is the moment `pick` went past `off`, left out while unset. Only an issue created after it is triaged, so turning triage on never reaches back over the backlog. Only `Runner::open` sets it, when `pick` is not `off` and none is saved, and clears it under `off`. `src/runner/triage.rs` (`settle_since`), `src/state/triage.rs`
- A triage record carries `at` (when it was made), `author`, `held` (the limit that held it) and `calls`, the moment of every triager call its issue made, each saved the moment its call ends, oldest first, the last `TRIAGE_CALLS_KEPT` kept (state version 69). A call counts when it reached a model, whatever its reply came to. A record a limit held, and one made from a triage comment found on the issue, hold no call. A record saved before them reads as no time, no author and no call, so it counts toward none. `src/state/triage.rs`
- The four limits are checked in order before an issue's research call, and none of them calls a model. `src/runner/triage/limits.rs`
  - `per_day`: at most this many calls in any 24 hours, counted from the calls the records save and the calls this run made on an issue with no record yet. Past it an issue waits in `triage.due` for the next free place and is never dropped. A cap above `MOST_PER_DAY` (200, the same as `TRIAGED_KEPT`) is refused at the settings check: that many finished records is all a 24 hour count can see.
  - `per_author_per_day`: the same count over one author's issues, logins compared ignoring case. Past it the issue gets `needs-triage` with no call and no comment, and its record says which limit held it.
  - `min_account_days`: an author whose account is younger than this and whose `author_association` is `NONE`, `FIRST_TIMER` or `FIRST_TIME_CONTRIBUTOR` is held the same way. The age is read once per author per start, and an age the forge cannot say holds nothing.
  - `max_body_kb`: the body the call is given is cut at that many kilobytes, on no character, with a line in the fence naming the size the whole body was. The title is never cut.
  - The maintainer's own issues and one `shep kelpie triage` asked for skip all four, which is why `triage_job` is told whether the limits reach it. A hold is final for that issue: its label and its record both keep it out of the due list, and only the verb reaches it again.
  - The author's cap and the account's age decide which of an author's issues kelpie takes on, so they reach an issue that has spent no call of its own. An issue kelpie is already working on is gated by the day's cap alone: a hold is final, and calls that came to nothing are no reason to label an issue over for good.
  - The author's round is gated by the day's cap as the first pass is, since its calls spend the same budget: a round call that finds no free place is left for the next poll, which reads the round from the records again. It is never held by a limit, because a hold would label over an issue already triaged.
  - An author the listing gives no login for, which is what an account deleted since the issue went up leaves, counts with no one: their records share no bucket, so they cannot spend each other's allowance.
- The pick rule runs once a `BOARD_POLL` while `pick` is not `off`, over `open_issues`. An issue is untouched when it is open and carries none of `TRIAGE_LABELS`, no bot opened it (the App included), it has no record in `ProjectState.triage.issues`, it was created after `triage.since`, and under `maintainer` `Runner::maintainer()` opened it, ignoring case.
- `status` lists them under `triage.due`, oldest first (number, title, author, author_association, created), left out when none; plain `status` adds a `triage due` line. The list lives in memory, rebuilt by the next poll. `src/runner/triage.rs`, `src/runner/trigger.rs`, `src/flock/control/plain.rs`
- `status` says the day's cap as `triage.daily` (`used`, `cap`, `opens`, `wait`) while triage and the cap are on, and plain `status` says the count against the cap and when the next place opens. A record a limit held is said in the plain block in the words `TriageHeld::said` gives it.
- A listing that cannot be read, or a maintainer that cannot be named, fails no poll.
- `shep kelpie triage <n>` queues `n` ahead of `triage.due`, past `since` and the author test. Refused when `pick` is `off`, when `n` is not open or carries a triage label, or when its call is in flight. A recorded issue is triaged again, its record replaced once the new comment posts.
- `status` lists the call in flight under `triage.running` and the records under `triage.issues`, a closed one with its `duplicate_of`.
