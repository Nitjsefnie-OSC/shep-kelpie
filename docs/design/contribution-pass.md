# The pass over a contribution's green head

How the reviewers in `contributions.reviewers` read a contribution, and what is recorded. Settings and detection are in `contributions.md`, the security read in `contribution-security.md`. #623

## The pass

- `Runner::run_pass` (`src/runner/contributions/pass.rs`) starts for a head that is `passed`, `is_green` and has no `pass`. It is a call in flight like the security read (`Job::Pass`, sharing `Flights.securing`): one contribution call at a time, a slot while it runs, #621's budget and order rules, after the security read in `contribution_due`.
- It walks `contributions.reviewers` from the start point. The first reviewer to answer, findings or `CLEAN`, ends the pass. Nothing is posted (#627).
- Rotation: the start point is the reviewer after `last_answered`, wrapping round, or the front with none. A push clears `pass` and keeps `last_answered` and `local_failures`, so the reviewers take turns over a contribution's pushes. A pass that ends unread leaves `last_answered` alone.
- Skipped: a reviewer with `paths` the change leaves alone (`touches`, from `changed_files` and `matches`, `src/runner/review/lineup.rs`), a local one that reviewed nothing twice (`local_failures`, `LOCAL_FAILURES_DOWN`), for one pass, after which its count restarts. A bot's turn is `contributions.md`'s, and a call reads the reviewers up to the next bot, then hands the walk back in `walking`. A reviewer whose call fails `ROUND_FAILURES` (3) times in a row is passed over. With no answer the pass ends unread and `pass.unread` names why. A call ended past the pass's one ceiling (15 minutes) ends it unread at once, with the cost kept, and the runner stopping leaves the head to the next poll. It is not retried until the next push.
- The reviewers read a detached worktree of the fetched `refs/pull/<n>/head` at `<project folder>/reading/<n>` (`worktree::contribution_checkout`, no hooks), after the agents' own files in it are checked against `main`'s (`fence::differ`): a difference ends the pass unread. A session gets the diff fenced as data and `Tools::Review` only; a local reviewer holds its lease as a work item's does. The worktree is removed when the pull request leaves the record.
- `contributions[].pass` is `{ head, ran, answered?, unread?, findings?, nits? }`, with `walking`, `last_answered` and `local_failures` beside it, all left out while empty. `nits` are a bot's open threads that are nits. State version 52.
