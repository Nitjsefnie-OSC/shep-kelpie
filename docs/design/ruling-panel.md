# The ruling panel

The experts that vote on a ruling, how a vote decides, and what the panel hands to the maintainer. Posting rulings as the App is in `github-app.md`.

- Once `agents.advisor` or `agents.researcher` set, ruling with 2+ options gets blind panel: worker, PM, advisor, researcher. #465
- A drain asks no expert: a ruling raised in one waits for the restart or `undrain` and takes its panel then. Still waiting after the hold, it goes to the maintainer with `skipped: draining` (also `posted`, and `repeated`), kept in `rulings.jsonl` and counted in `status`. #711 A question put to the PM stays the PM's through a drain; the panel does not take it up. A question the panel already decided that the worker asks again, in the same words, skips the panel (`skipped: repeated`) and goes to the maintainer, since the decision did not answer it. #1250
- Question listing under 2 options: PM's one wake drafts 2-3 `candidates` (one may be `Ask the maintainer`, a vote for it, or for a candidate that begins "You", decides nothing: only the maintainer can carry it out), which become its `- ` options, then the panel votes as on any ruling. Question text stays as asked; free text still answers. No PM or no panel = no candidates. #709, `runner/pm/question.rs`
- Comment waits up to 20 min for votes (`panel::HOLD`), each expert 15 min. Vote `{"choice", "why", "design"}`; malformed abstains. #465, `runner/flight/panel.rs`
- Advisor (Opus 5.5 high) reads codebase; researcher (Sonnet 5.5 high) reads past rulings, threads, transcripts, web limited to `researcher.allowed_domains` (page may steer it). Pinned; fetch dials the page host; no key. pi: `pi-web-access` (`adapters/pi/web.rs`), search host `mcp.exa.ai`. goose: `duckduckgo-websearch` MCP `kelpie-web` (`adapters/goose/web.rs`), search host `html.duckduckgo.com`. No package: reads only, noted. #465, #767, #797, `crates/core/src/transcripts/mod.rs`
- `rulings.decide`: `shadow` (default), `panel` (3 agreeing votes, no `design` flag decide; splits and merges under `ask` go to maintainer) or `always` (see budget.md). Reopen within 24h with `<id> no <note>`. #466, `runner/panel/decided.rs`
- PR title type sets who votes, smallest panel first. Questions and diffs touching `rulings.full_panel_paths` get full panel (agent under scrutiny chose the type). #466, #536, `crates/panel/src/panel/`
- Under `shadow`, a ruling answered while its panel votes keeps the panel running to the end of its hold. The votes are written to the ruling's line in `rulings.jsonl` and count against the answer. The answer takes effect at once: the worker's vote, which shares the work item's session, is ended, and the rest go on. The worker abstains with the reason that the maintainer answered first, kept as `overtaken` on the line and counted apart from `abstained` in `status`. Under `panel` and `always` an answer ends the vote. #725, `runner/panel/`
- Policies scored from shadow votes as `whatif`: fixed list in code, not a setting (a setting can't say what a choice costs). #536
- `rulings.pm_alone`: PM decides listed kinds alone, never `merge` (one voice must not merge) and never `security` (a stranger's text must not reach a vote). #466, `crates/core/src/settings/rulings.rs`
- An option can name an action kelpie runs in code when chosen (rerun, restore); the experts vote on its text and stay read-only. See merge.md. #710
- Votes are ledger entries; `status` reports agreement with maintainer (trust by record). #466, `runner/panel/measure/`
