# Cost by role and worker questions

- Cost by role, built in #70: a work item's calls record the worker's and each review session's, each with its cost, and `status` and the finished item's log line total them by role, with the qwen rounds as a count and seconds.
- A worker may end its `<kelpie-question>` block with two to four `- ` lines of likely answers (built in #79). They stay inside the question verbatim, so the ruling, its comment and the webhook show them too.
