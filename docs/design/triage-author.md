# The author's round

An open question goes to the issue's author before it goes to the maintainer (#887, #892, #893, #1108): one round, answered in a comment on GitHub or in the author's own terminal, and `needs-triage` for what is left. The settings, the pick rule, the verb and `status` are in `triage.md`; the pass, its post and its labels are in `triage-pass.md`.

- `Runner::askable` is who is asked: the maintainer on their own issue, and any author under `pick = "all"`. Anyone else ends at `needs-triage` as before. `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- Asked and not sure: one comment holds the representative's lead-in, the questions and, below them, the local prompt inside a collapsed `<details>` section. No status label goes on while the triage waits. `Runner::finish_triage` returns at a record whose `questions` or `local` are open, and the type and priority the pass owed wait in its `labels`. `Runner::ask_comment` builds the body, `runner/triage/comment.rs`, `runner/triage/local.rs`
- `Runner::author_role` (`runner/triage/ask.rs`) is what an author may be asked, tried in this order: the role `triage.authors` lists for their login, `maintainer` for `Runner::maintainer()`, else the one their association implies (`AuthorRole::of`: `OWNER` maintainer, `MEMBER`, `COLLABORATOR` and `CONTRIBUTOR` developer, anything else, an association the listing did not say included, reporter). The role fixes the call's question cap (`AuthorRole::most_questions`: `max_questions` for a maintainer, at most 3 for a developer, 1 for a reporter, never past `max_questions`), which `read_reply` enforces on the reply, and the call prompt names the association and the role (`research::prompt`). `agents/triager.md` sets what each may be asked. For any role but `maintainer` the files, tests and criteria stay out of the facts the representative words from and out of the local prompt, so nothing posted to them names a path. A pass that lost its role to a restart is worded as for a reporter. #1109
- The questions are kelpie's code, not the representative's words: the triager's `open`, at most `max_questions`, numbered, each with its recommended answer, in the vendored grilling skill's shape (`skills/skills/productivity/grilling`) without its bold. A backtick or tilde fence in a question or answer is dropped, so it cannot swallow the section below. `questions_block`, `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- `triage.issues` gains `questions {comment, at}` (state version 67) and `local {at, body}` (state version 68), each left out while unset. Both are set when the comment goes up, and either ends the wait. `at` is when the comment went up and is what silence is measured from. `asked {at, asks}` is no longer written. A file saved on it still loads, and `answers_wait` reads its wait as the answers wait on the triage comment. `crates/core/src/state/triage.rs`
- Hearing: `read_threads` reads while any triage waits, `Runner::triage_waits` putting each wait's start into the read's `since` and holding the read's floor there while a wait is open, so a busy forge or a held slot does not drop the reply, and `Runner::hear_triage` keeps only what the issue's own author wrote on that issue after the comment went up, by login through `plain_login`. A bot, the App's own post and anyone else's words are dropped. The login is the pass's, taken from the open listing each poll so a restart has it. `runner/thread_replies/`, `runner/triage/heard.rs`
- Any comment from the author is their answers, whatever it says: no word is read from it. A comment that opens with `github` or `local`, or answers only some questions, still starts the second pass, which reads it against the questions and asks again only what it left unsettled. `runner/representative/reading.rs` is no longer used by triage.
- The author's comments after the triage comment are the answers. A second triager call (`Purpose::Answers`) reads the issue, the questions and the answers, each fenced, and answers again. The maintainer's own issue takes the agent-ready branch of `triage-pass.md`. An outside issue gets what is settled written under its author's own text in its body, never in place of it: the `answers` step, with the whole body in `ready_body`. `Runner::settled_answers`, `Runner::answers_write`. What is still open is listed in that comment, and there is no second round. A body the forge cannot be read for is not stood for by a comment and labels: the pass runs again. A write a hook refused keeps the type and priority the pass found.
- No comment from the author `triage.wait_days` after the triage comment: the labels the pass owed go on, then `needs-triage`, and nothing is posted, so the triage comment stays the maintainer's starting point. No second ask is posted. `Runner::triage_none`
- The local prompt is a fenced block kelpie's code builds: `/grill-with-docs Issue <owner/repo>#<n>: <title>.`, reading the issue with `gh issue view <n> --comments`, the files the triage comment named, the open questions each with what the research recommends, and the write-back that is how kelpie learns what was settled: rewrite the body with acceptance criteria and a `Touches:` list, and comment the decisions made. The title, every file and every question with its recommended answer lose backticks and line breaks, so none of them can close the block or stand as a line of the prompt. The section says where the skill is: `grill-with-docs` from mattpocock/skills, kelpie's vendored copy at `skills/skills/engineering/grill-with-docs`. `runner/triage/local.rs`
- `local {at, body}` keeps the body kelpie last saw as a FNV-1a hash of its trimmed text, so a wait begun on one body is never read against another. Kelpie's own body writes set it (`Runner::triage_body_written`, called where `Runner::answers_write` and `Runner::ready_write` write), so a write of kelpie's own is not the author's edit. The hash is taken from a read made just after the representative words the comment, since that call can run for minutes. `runner/triage/local.rs`
- Each `BOARD_POLL`, `Runner::watch_local_waits` reads each issue waiting on `local` through `Tickets::issue`. A body whose hash differs starts the triager call again on the new text (`Purpose::LocalRead`), and its comment ends the round: the maintainer's own issue the pass is sure of takes the agent-ready branch of `triage-pass.md`, any other gets `needs-triage`, and what is still open goes to the maintainer with no further ask. The wait stays open until that comment lands, so a restart between the edit and its call reads the body again and starts the call again. No read runs under a call in flight on the issue. That call is made on the body this read saw, and `Runner::local_read_stale` reads the body again before its comment goes up: a body rewritten a second time under the call posts nothing and writes no body, and is owed again on the newer text. Silence ends here too, after the body is read: no edit and no comment `triage.wait_days` after the comment, with no answers heard, is `none`. `hear_triage` ends silence only for a wait saved with no prompt.
- The author answers in a comment or by rewriting the body. A comment starts the answers call, and when both are there the comment is read first. Either way the round ends with that call, and the record drops both waits.
- A triage label put on by hand, or the issue closed, ends the wait with no post and no label: `Runner::settle_triage_waits` reads the listing each poll, and `Runner::triage_wait_stood` saves the record `done` with nothing owed.
- The wait holds no slot and no call of its own. The call it starts is taken from `Runner::triage_round_due`, after the same pick as the first, and takes a slot as the first does.
- No wait is settled, read or asked for under a triager call in flight on the same issue, and a given-up issue is neither heard from nor settled until kelpie restarts. `Runner::answers_round` returning none is no call. `runner/triage/ask.rs`, `runner/triage/heard.rs`
