# Triage

Kelpie's triage of issues other people open (#887): the settings, the agent, the pick rule, the verb and `status`. One issue's pass, its duplicate close and its agent-ready branch are in `triage-pass.md`.

- `[app.dogs.kelpie.triage]`, an absent table meaning `pick = "off"`, with `deny_unknown_fields`. `src/settings/triage.rs`
  - `pick`: `off` (default), `maintainer` (only the maintainer's issues) or `all`
  - `wait_days`: whole days above 0, default 7
  - `max_questions`: 1 to 10, default 10, refused above 10 by `Triage::check`.
  - `agent`: an agent file whose role is `triager`, default `triager`. `Settings::triager` checks it.
- `reread` keeps the running `triage` as waiting for the next start, as it does `contributions`. `src/runner/reread.rs`
- Agent file `triager.md`, role `triager`, `Expert`-shaped frontmatter, shipped on Claude Code and written by `add`. It researches an issue against a clean checkout and reports in one JSON object: `type`, `priority`, `related`, `addressed_by`, `files`, `tests`, `criteria`, `open` and `body`, the last a rewrite of the issue text for an agent. The issue text is data. `agents/triager.md`
- Every harness gives the triager the reviewer's read-only reach (`Tools::Review`), per `harness.triager` in `docs/parity.toml`.
- `Role::Triager` in `src/agents.rs` and `crate::ports::Role`. No work item records its calls (`src/work_item/spend.rs`, `tally.rs`). The code graph reaches it when on (`reads_code`, `src/runner/code_graph.rs`), with its own index (`ProjectPaths::triage_graph`), removed when the call ends. `CallKind::Triage` gives it a `triager` total in `usage`, apart from the work items. `src/usage.rs`
- `Runner::open` checks the settings in `src/runner/open_checks.rs`. While `triage.pick` is not `off`, the App must cover the repo (as `contributions.pick` does), then `triage.agent` and `agents.representative` must name files with the right role. Under `off` no file is read.
- `OpenIssue` carries `author`, `author_is_bot`, `labels` and `created` from `gh issue list`. `src/ports/issue.rs`, `src/adapters/gh/issues.rs`
- `ProjectState.triage { since }` (state version 60) is the moment `pick` went past `off`, left out while unset. Only an issue created after it is triaged, so turning triage on never reaches back over the backlog. Only `Runner::open` sets it, when `pick` is not `off` and none is saved, and clears it under `off`. `src/runner/triage.rs` (`settle_since`), `src/state/triage.rs`
- The pick rule runs once a `BOARD_POLL` while `pick` is not `off`, over `open_issues`. An issue is untouched when it is open and carries none of `TRIAGE_LABELS`, no bot opened it (the App included), it has no record in `ProjectState.triage.issues`, it was created after `triage.since`, and under `maintainer` `Runner::maintainer()` opened it, ignoring case.
- `status` lists them under `triage.due`, oldest first (number, title, author, created), left out when none; plain `status` adds a `triage due` line. The list lives in memory, rebuilt by the next poll. `src/runner/triage.rs`, `src/runner/trigger.rs`, `src/flock/control/plain.rs`
- A listing that cannot be read, or a maintainer that cannot be named, fails no poll.
- `shep kelpie triage <n>` queues `n` ahead of `triage.due`, past `since` and the author test. Refused when `pick` is `off`, when `n` is not open or carries a triage label, or when its call is in flight. A recorded issue is triaged again, its record replaced once the new comment posts.
- `status` lists the call in flight under `triage.running` and the records under `triage.issues`, a closed one with its `duplicate_of`.

## The author's round

An open question goes to the issue's author before it goes to the maintainer (#892). One round of questions on GitHub, then `needs-triage` for what is left.

- `Runner::askable` is who is asked: the maintainer on their own issue, and any author under `pick = "all"`. Anyone else ends at `needs-triage` as before. `src/runner/triage/ask.rs`
- Asked and not sure: the comment's facts end asking the author to settle the open questions, `github` or `none`, and no status label goes on while the triage waits. `Runner::finish_triage` returns at a record whose `asked` or `questions` are open, and the type and priority the pass owed wait in its `labels`. `src/runner/triage/comment.rs`
- `triage.issues` gains `asked {at, asks}` and `questions {comment, at}` (state version 67), each left out while unset. `at` is when the ask or the questions went up and is what silence is measured from; `asks` is 1, or 2 once the plain ask went up, and is left out while 1. `src/state/triage.rs`
- Hearing: `read_threads` reads while any triage waits, `Runner::triage_waits` putting each wait's start into the read's `since` and holding the read's floor there while a wait is open, so a busy forge or a held slot does not drop the reply, and `Runner::hear_triage` keeps only what the issue's own author wrote on that issue after the wait began, by login through `plain_login`. A bot, the App's own post and anyone else's words are dropped. The login is the pass's, taken from the open listing each poll so a restart has it. `src/runner/thread_replies.rs`, `src/runner/triage/heard.rs`
- Reading: `src/runner/representative/reading.rs` is the one shape a reply is read through. `kind` words what the call is for from the words the caller gave, and `read` keeps only one of them or `other`; the reply text is fenced by `represent_issue`, so it is data and never instructions. `contributions/release.rs` reads `yes`, `no` or `other` through it, and triage reads `github`, `none` or `other`. A word read for a comment is kept in the pass, so a post that failed is not paid for again.
- Each reply found is read, oldest first, and the first word kelpie can read is what it acts on. A reply that is neither word gets one plain comment asking again (`<!-- kelpie:triage ask -->`, `Runner::triage_ask_again`), which sets `asks` to 2 and moves `at` to itself. A second reply kelpie cannot read counts as `none`.
- `none`, or no word `triage.wait_days` after the ask: the labels the pass owed go on, then `needs-triage`, and nothing is posted, so the triage comment stays the maintainer's starting point. `Runner::triage_none`
- `github`: one comment (`<!-- kelpie:triage questions -->`) whose lead-in the representative words and whose questions kelpie's code lists from the triager's `open`, at most `max_questions`, numbered, each with its recommended answer, in the vendored grilling skill's shape (`skills/skills/productivity/grilling`) without its bold. `Runner::post_questions`. A `github` whose questions a restart lost is researched again before anything is posted (`Purpose::Questions`).
- The author's comments after the questions are the answers. A second triager call (`Purpose::Answers`) reads the issue, the questions and the answers, each fenced, and answers again. The maintainer's own issue takes the agent-ready branch of `triage-pass.md`. An outside issue gets what is settled written under its author's own text in its body, never in place of it: the `answers` step, with the whole body in `ready_body`. `Runner::settled_answers`, `Runner::answers_write`. What is still open is listed in that comment, and there is no second round. A body the forge cannot be read for is not stood for by a comment and labels: the pass runs again. A write a hook refused keeps the type and priority the pass found.
- No answer `triage.wait_days` after the questions ends the way `none` does.
- A triage label put on by hand, or the issue closed, ends the wait with no post and no label: `Runner::settle_triage_waits` reads the listing each poll, and `Runner::triage_wait_stood` saves the record `done` with nothing owed.
- The wait holds no slot and no call. The second call starts from `Runner::triage_round_due`, after the same pick as the first, and takes a slot as the first does.
- No wait is settled, read or asked for under a triager call in flight on the same issue, and a given-up issue is neither heard from nor settled until kelpie restarts. `Runner::answers_round` returning none is no call. `src/runner/triage/ask.rs`, `src/runner/triage/heard.rs`
